Before your visit

A few little
things to know.

How do I book?

Call 01427 617211. We book by phone so we can help you choose the right treatment, technician and appointment.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

New clients are asked for a 50% non-refundable deposit to secure their appointment. If you can’t make it, please give us as much notice as possible—it gives us a chance to offer your time to someone else.

What is BIAB?

Builder in a Bottle is a builder gel used to add structure and strength to natural nails. Ask us whether it suits your nails and the finish you want.

When should I book my infill?

We recommend discussing your next appointment with your technician. Our usual infill interval is around two to three weeks, depending on your nails and treatment.

Do I need a patch test?

Our eye treatments are patch-tested as standard. Please call before your appointment so we can arrange the appropriate test and timing.

Where can I park?

Tesco on Trinity Street is a short walk away. Please check the signs for current parking terms and time limits before leaving your car.

How can I pay?

We accept cash or bank transfer. There’s an ATM at nearby Tesco if you need it.
